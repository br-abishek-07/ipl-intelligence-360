# Captaincy source requirement

Cricsheet match JSON `info.players` indicates players involved, **not** the captain or confirmed pre-match XI. The V2-C dashboard therefore explicitly withholds best-captain tables.

To activate future captaincy metrics, compile one captain per franchise-match in the template CSV with a verifiable scorecard or official team-sheet citation. Cross-check match_id against the IPL master and verify the player identity in Cricsheet registry.

Proposed leadership KPIs: decided matches captained; wins and win percentage (minimum 20 matches); final wins; championship titles as captain (only when match-by-match captain is independently verified). Do not merge on name alone or assume longest-tenured player was captain.
