# V2-C Sports Analyst Research Notes

**Provenance:** IPL Cricsheet-derived match, innings and player tables bundled in this project. Results describe this dataset, including 2026 records.

## Key boundaries
- Franchise histories standardize official rebrands (RCB/Bangalore vs Bengaluru; PBKS/Kings XI; DC/Delhi Daredevils). Historical defunct franchises remain separate.
- All-time trophies are counted only from actual match-type Final winners, not inferred standings.
- Bowler wicket leaders use **credited wickets**; team-phase wickets include all dismissals.
- Cricsheet participant lists do not certify captains or starting XIs, therefore best-captain claims remain unavailable.
- Team and player rankings change with the page slicers; all-time championships are intentionally persistent.
